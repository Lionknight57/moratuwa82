It is with deep sadness that we share the news of the passing of the mother of
our batchmate **[Priyantha Perera](/members/)**.

## The funeral

Her remains lie at the **family residence near Dagonna Church**.

The funeral will be held on **Monday 7 September at 3.30 p.m.** at
**Dagonna Church**.

A [location map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VRyQRhW25jqaKv5U7) was shared with the
notice.

## Condolences

Priyantha has asked that condolences be conveyed to him directly. Batchmates who
need his number will find it in the batch WhatsApp group, where Lakshman posted
the notice on 6 September.

*May her soul rest in peace.*
